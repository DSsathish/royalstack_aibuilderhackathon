Three gaps in every neighbourhood shop

In the aisle

Nobody to ask

Shoppers hunt for items, get no answer in their own language, and end the trip in a queue.

In the back office

Guesswork

Owners reorder from memory, hear about demand only after the sale is lost, and find shrink at stock-take.

In the software

Islands of stock

The billing system holds one count, the online store another, and neither can talk to a shopper.

Self-checkout without verification raised losses for big chains; small shops never got the assistant, the forecast or the sync at all.
