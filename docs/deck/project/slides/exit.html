Verdicts

Clear, spot check, or flag. Staff review flags on a tablet with a kiosk PIN that locks after failed attempts.

Privacy by design

The model never blocks an exit on its own. Photos are processed in memory; only a hash, a confidence and item names are kept.

Exit verification · loss prevention without a guard at the door

Deterministic rules decide; Gemini vision only moves the confidence. That is the answer to the self-checkout shrink problem and to the privacy question in one design.
