In the store: scan a code, talk, scan, pay, leave

01 · Entrance QR

No install. Phone sign-in is optional and unlocks history, offers and points.

02 · Talk, in your language

Gemini Live speech-to-speech, interruptible, with memory across visits. Text works the same.

03 · The map lights up

“Where is atta?” highlights the aisle and bay; aisle QRs update where you stand.

04 · Scan-first cart

Items enter only by barcode scan or a confirmed photo match, so the receipt reflects what was handled.

05 · Offers and points

Owner-made offers apply themselves, best one wins. Points redeem after a fresh code, one rupee each.

06 · Pay and leave

Sandbox payment, a signed receipt QR, and a running receipt that was visible the whole time.

Shopper experience · in store and online

Walk the six steps. The lesson from cashierless stores is that shoppers want to see the bill as it grows, so the receipt is always on screen. Scan-first is a deliberate choice: the exit check compares against what was physically scanned.
