For the owner: inventory that thinks ahead

Demand planning

BigQuery ML ARIMA_PLUS per product, retrained nightly for every store; days of cover and dead stock on one screen.

Reorders with a reason

Drafts grouped by supplier with a Gemini-written rationale. The owner approves, edits or rejects; nothing is sent without them.

Demand signals

“Asked but not carried” and “asked but out of stock”, captured from conversations and searches, with lost-sale estimates.

Customer insight

Signed-in shoppers in segments: new, regular, VIP, at risk. Last four digits only; preferences, never conversations.

Offers and points

Product, category, bill-threshold and segment offers that apply themselves. A points ledger the owner can adjust, every move audited.

Loss and fraud

Exit verdicts, shrink from shelf counts against system stock, audit rate and limits the owner controls.

And the same assistant, by voice: “What is running low?”, “How much milk will we sell this week?”, “Prepare purchase orders.”
